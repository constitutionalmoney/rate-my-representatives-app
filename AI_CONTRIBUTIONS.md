# AI-Assisted Contributions

AI tools may assist development, research, documentation, testing, and review. They do not replace contributor responsibility.

## Contributor responsibility

The human contributor who submits a change is responsible for:

- understanding the submitted code and documentation;
- confirming that the contribution is accurate and complete enough for its stated purpose;
- confirming that the contributor has the right to submit all included material;
- identifying and complying with third-party licences;
- preventing secrets, personal information, private civic activity, copyrighted source dumps, or confidential material from entering prompts, outputs, commits, issues, logs, or tests;
- testing security, privacy, accessibility, mobile, API, database, and Verus behavior affected by the change;
- signing the commit under the DCO; and
- correcting defects found after submission.

“An AI generated it” is not a provenance record, legal defence, test result, or review.

## Permitted uses

AI may help:

- draft code from an accepted issue or specification;
- generate test cases and fixtures using synthetic data;
- identify possible edge cases;
- draft documentation for human verification;
- refactor code while preserving tested behavior;
- classify or summarize public technical material with source attribution;
- compare API contracts and implementation; and
- prepare a pull-request description.

## Prohibited or restricted uses

Do not use AI to:

- invent sources, records, representatives, offices, terms, evidence, results, legal conclusions, approvals, or test outcomes;
- cast, change, or infer a representative signal or category rating;
- impersonate a citizen, representative, staff member, moderator, committee member, or maintainer;
- publish an allegation or disputed conclusion without the required human decision;
- create hidden ideology, conformity, loyalty, eligibility, or social-credit profiles;
- process production private keys, seed phrases, WIFs, wallet files, identity evidence, precise addresses, private representative signals, confidential moderation records, or non-public security reports;
- silently alter Light Mathematics factors, weights, exclusions, confidence rules, or methodology versions;
- approve a Verus Mobile identity update, wallet signature, official response, correction request, or release gate on behalf of a human; or
- represent generated output as independently verified.

## Source and licence review

AI output may resemble licensed code or prose. Search for distinctive copied passages when risk is material. Do not submit code with unknown provenance, fabricated attributions, incompatible licence terms, or copied secrets.

Record third-party material in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and preserve required notices.

## Review evidence

For a material AI-assisted pull request, identify:

- the portions materially generated or transformed;
- the tool or model family when disclosure is permitted;
- the specification or issue used as the source of requirements;
- tests performed;
- source and licence checks performed; and
- any known uncertainty requiring reviewer attention.

Do not paste private prompts or proprietary model output merely to prove use. Provide the reviewable reasoning, sources, tests, and change history instead.

## Product AI versus development AI

This policy governs development contributions. Runtime Civic Agents and Civic Signal research features have separate product restrictions: they may assist source discovery, classification, extraction, duplicate detection, neutral drafting, and coverage analysis, but they cannot manufacture human civic intent or bypass moderation.
