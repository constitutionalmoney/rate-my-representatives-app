# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Rate My Representatives project is committed to a collaborative environment where people can scrutinize public institutions, technical designs, evidence, methods, and project decisions without harassment, intimidation, discrimination, partisan gatekeeping, or personal retaliation.

This commitment applies to contributors, maintainers, reviewers, contractors, representatives, researchers, community members, and anyone acting in an official project space or representing the project publicly.

## Expected conduct

Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas, evidence, code, policy, and public records rather than attacking people;
- distinguish fact, source, inference, opinion, allegation, and uncertainty;
- provide specific, reproducible feedback;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- respect privacy, consent, correction, appeal, and security procedures;
- avoid publishing personal information, private civic activity, credentials, or vulnerability details;
- accept that political disagreement is not misconduct by itself;
- use project terminology accurately, including the distinction between Civic Signal, representative signals, evidence, ratings, official responses, and methodology outputs;
- respect accessibility needs and different communication styles;
- comply with repository licensing, DCO, third-party rights, and contributor policies; and
- follow maintainer instructions intended to protect safety, scope, and review integrity.

## Unacceptable conduct

Unacceptable conduct includes:

- harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, or dehumanizing language;
- discrimination or targeted hostility based on identity, disability, nationality, citizenship, political belief, religion, age, sex, gender, sexual orientation, or other protected/personal characteristics;
- doxxing or publishing private contact, location, identity, family, health, financial, employment, civic-activity, or security information;
- encouraging violence or retaliation;
- sexualized attention or content directed at another participant;
- impersonating a contributor, citizen, representative, staff member, moderator, maintainer, or organization;
- knowingly fabricating sources, evidence, votes, offices, identities, quotes, approvals, tests, or project status;
- coordinated manipulation, brigading, spam, or using synthetic identities to manufacture civic sentiment;
- attempting to expose individual representative signals, ratings, subscriptions, identity evidence, or moderator records;
- using project systems or community spaces to create social-credit, loyalty, ideology, or generalized citizen-reputation profiles;
- submitting secrets, private keys, seed phrases, WIFs, wallet files, identity documents, or confidential evidence to public project spaces;
- publishing an unpatched vulnerability or exploit instructions outside the coordinated security process;
- bad-faith legal threats or demands intended to suppress legitimate technical or source-based review; and
- sustained disruption after a maintainer has given a clear scope or conduct direction.

## Political and evidentiary neutrality

The project may contain strong criticism of public officials, institutions, laws, policies, parties, or governance systems. Such criticism is permitted when it complies with project rules.

The Code of Conduct must not be used to:

- suppress a contributor merely because their political conclusions differ;
- treat criticism of a government or representative as harassment without additional conduct;
- convert ideological agreement into maintainer privilege;
- bypass evidence, correction, appeal, or governance processes; or
- protect a public claim from good-faith source-based challenge.

## Scope

This Code applies in:

- issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, commits, and project documentation;
- official chat, video, event, mailing, support, and moderation spaces;
- private project communications where conduct affects project participation or safety; and
- public conduct by a person explicitly representing the project.

It does not make the project the general regulator of a participant's unrelated private or public life.

## Reporting

Do not report a security vulnerability through this conduct process; follow `SECURITY.md`.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's or project's designated private contact route. Do not include unnecessary personal information, identity evidence, credentials, private civic activity, or vulnerability details.

A useful report identifies:

- what happened and where;
- dates or approximate times;
- relevant links or screenshots with unrelated personal data removed;
- people directly involved or affected;
- immediate safety concerns; and
- conflicts that could affect who reviews the report.

## Enforcement

Maintainers or the project steward may take proportionate action, including:

1. clarification or private warning;
2. removal or editing of harmful content while preserving necessary audit history;
3. temporary interaction limits;
4. removal from a role or project space;
5. permanent ban from official project spaces; and
6. escalation to platform, legal, or emergency channels when required.

Decisions should consider severity, intent, impact, pattern, safety, evidence, conflicts, corrective action, and risk of recurrence. A person materially involved in the dispute should not be the sole decision-maker.

Where appropriate and safe, the person subject to action should receive the rule applied, a concise reason, duration/scope, and an appeal route. Appeals are not guaranteed to disclose private reporter information or security-sensitive evidence.

## Stewardship

Checks and Balances Committee Ltd. is the steward of the official repository and appoints maintainers responsible for enforcing this Code within delegated authority.

Enforcement must remain consistent with `GOVERNANCE.md`, `SECURITY.md`, the No Social Credit Covenant, and applicable law. Project access or maintainer status does not create employment, equity, compensation, fiduciary status, or public authority unless separately agreed in writing.
