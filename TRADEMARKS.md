## Permitted descriptive use

You may make truthful statements such as:

- “This software is based on the open-source Rate My Representatives repository.”
- “This is an independent fork of Rate My Representatives.”

Descriptive use must not be more prominent than your own distinct product name and must not suggest official status.

Forks must use distinct names, logos, application identifiers, signing certificates, domains, API hosts, trust roots, and app-store listings. They must clearly disclose that they are independent and identify material changes.

A fork may not copy an official-status badge or configure software in a way that causes users, wallets, representatives, contributors, or relying parties to believe it is an official deployment.
